# Unit Economics & ROI

If Credex deployed this tool tomorrow, here is the economic breakdown of how it functions as a lead-generation asset.

### 1. Value of a Converted Lead (LTV)
- **Estimate:** ~$1,200 per converted lead in Year 1.
- **Reasoning:** Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. A typical Seed/Series A startup spending $1,000/mo on AI tools might buy $12,000 worth of annual credits through Credex at a 20% discount ($9,600 actual spend). If Credex operates on a 15% margin on those brokered credits, Credex makes roughly $1,440 in gross profit per year per converted startup. Let's conservatively estimate $1,200 LTV for the first year.

### 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Channel
- **Hacker News / Organic Social:** $0 CAC (excluding the sunk cost of my engineering time to build the tool).
- **Cold Outbound Email (to CTOs):** ~$15 per audit completed.
  - *Math:* Apollo.io/similar data provider + email warm-up costs ~$100/mo. Sending 1,000 emails yields a 2% conversion to completing an audit = 20 audits. ($100 / 20 = $5 per audit). However, time spent personalizing increases effective CAC to ~$15.
- **Targeted Twitter/LinkedIn Ads (Future):** ~$40 per completed audit.

### 3. Conversion Funnel to Profitability
For the tool to be profitable, the funnel math must work. Let's trace a cohort of 1,000 cold visitors:
- **Landing Page Visitors:** 1,000
- **Audits Completed:** 200 (20% conversion, high because it's ungated and free)
- **High-Savings Leads Identified (>$500/mo savings):** 40 (20% of audits)
- **Email Captured & Consultation Booked:** 8 (20% of high-savings leads opt-in to talk to Credex)
- **Credit Purchase (Closed Won):** 2 (25% close rate on consultations)

*Result:* 1,000 visitors yields 2 closed deals. 
*Revenue:* 2 deals × $1,200 LTV = $2,400 gross profit.
If we acquired those 1,000 visitors via paid ads at $1 CPC ($1,000 total spend), we net $1,440 profit. If acquired organically, it is pure margin.

### 4. Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To generate $1,000,000 in ARR strictly from leads generated by this tool, the following must be true:
- At $1,200 LTV, we need **833 closed deals** in 18 months (~46 deals per month).
- Based on our funnel (0.2% visitor-to-close rate), we need **23,000 unique visitors per month** to the tool.
- **What has to be true:** Organic virality is not enough to sustain 23k visitors/mo consistently. We would need to implement the "Bonus" features (embeddable widgets for tech bloggers, referral codes where startups get an extra 5% discount for referring another founder) to create a viral loop, supplemented by a sustainable paid acquisition engine where CAC is strictly <$300 per closed deal.
