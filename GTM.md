# Go-To-Market Strategy (GTM)

**Exact Target User:**
The primary target user is a **Founding CTO or VP of Engineering** at a Seed or Series A B2B SaaS company (10–50 employees). These individuals hold the credit card for infrastructure, have approved individual AI tool requests out of necessity over the last 18 months, and now have a bloated, fragmented software bill they haven't had time to audit.

**Pre-Trigger Mindset (What they Google/Scroll):**
Right before wanting this tool, they are likely looking at their Ramp or Brex dashboard at the end of the month, sighing at the SaaS spend. They are Googling things like "GitHub Copilot vs Cursor team ROI," "Claude API vs ChatGPT Enterprise cost," or scrolling through Hacker News threads titled "Ask HN: What is your startup's AI stack?" or "Are we overpaying for OpenAI API?"

**Where they hang out online:**
- **Specific Subreddits:** r/SaaS, r/startups, r/macapps (lots of overlap with dev tool early adopters).
- **Slack/Discord:** Y Combinator Startup School Slack, Techstars alumni network, local tech hub Slacks.
- **X (Twitter) Lists:** Lists tracking "Indie Hackers," "Technical Founders," or followers of prominent devtool builders like Guillermo Rauch or Gergely Orosz.

**Acquiring the first 100 users in 30 days ($0 budget):**
1. **The "Show HN" Launch:** A meticulously crafted "Show HN: I built an engine to audit your startup's AI overspend" post. Hacker News loves utility tools that save money, hate bloated enterprise software, and appreciate transparent calculations.
2. **Cold Email with a "Teaser Audit":** Scrape a list of 50 Seed-stage CTOs from recent Crunchbase funding rounds. Send a highly personalized, text-only email: *"Hey [Name], saw [Company] just raised a Seed. I built a free tool that finds overlapping AI spend (like devs using both Cursor and Copilot). Most teams your size waste ~$400/mo. Run your stack through it here: [URL]."*
3. **The 'Roast My Stack' Twitter Thread:** Post an anonymized screenshot of a terrible AI stack (e.g., a solo founder paying for 4 different $20/mo subscriptions) with the caption "Roast this AI stack." Follow up with the tool link in the replies.

**The Unfair Distribution Channel:**
Because this is a lead-generation tool for **Credex**, the unfair advantage is Credex's existing inventory of discounted credits. The audit engine can dynamically say: *"You're paying $39/mo for Copilot Enterprise. We actually have unused Copilot Enterprise seats from a pivoted startup we can sell you right now for $25/mo."* No other generic audit tool can instantly broker the discounted alternative.

**Week-1 Traction Expectations:**
If this works, Week 1 traction looks like:
- 1,200 unique page views (driven largely by the HN launch or a mildly viral tweet).
- 250 audits completed (a ~20% conversion rate from landing to finishing the form, since it requires no login).
- 40 emails captured (16% of completed audits input their email because they saw >$100/mo savings).
- 5 qualified high-savings leads booked for a Credex consultation.
